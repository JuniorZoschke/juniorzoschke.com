How it works

Four simple steps

  1. A confidential chat

    An informal conversation, by phone or in person, about you and your plans.

  2. Understanding the business

    I learn how the business works and what matters to you, quietly, and at your pace.

  3. Offer and agreement

    A clear offer and a plain-English agreement, set out in writing.

  4. A careful handover

    A handover period we agree together, so staff, customers and suppliers can keep working as usual.

How I set out a purchase

Every deal is set out in writing, in plain English, before you commit to anything. That includes what you're paid when the sale goes through and what (if anything) is paid later. I'll show you how the business can support the deal, so you can see it works for your team as well as for you. I encourage you to take your own legal and financial advice, and I'm happy to talk with your advisers directly. I'll always give you an honest answer, fast.
